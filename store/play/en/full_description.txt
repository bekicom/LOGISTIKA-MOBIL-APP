FURAM is a freight platform for Uzbekistan and the CIS. Cargo owners, carriers, drivers and dispatchers work in one app.

LOADS AND TRUCKS
• Load board and available trucks with filters by country and region
• Listings from Telegram groups gathered in one feed
• Saved searches show how many new offers have appeared

TRIPS
• A trip from agreement to closing: documents, expenses, stages
• Live route for the cargo owner and dispatcher — only during a trip and only with your permission
• SOS button

CHAT WITHOUT A LANGUAGE BARRIER
• Messages are translated into the other person's language (8 languages)
• Voice messages turn into text
• Block and report users

AI ASSISTANT
• Finds loads and trucks, explains a contract, sums up a trip
• Works only with your consent (powered by OpenAI)

FLEET AND FINANCE
• Vehicles, drivers and document expiry reminders
• Expense tracking and receipt scanning
• Income and expense analytics

AND MORE
• Repair shops and spare parts, vehicle sales, jobs for drivers
• Trust rating of participants
• 8 interface languages: Uzbek, Russian, English, Turkish, Kazakh, Kyrgyz, Tajik, Chinese
